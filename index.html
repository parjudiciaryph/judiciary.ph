About the courts

Background information supplied for this project.

Supreme Court

The Supreme Court is the highest court of the Judiciary, headed by the Chief Justice and up to six Associate Justices. The Chief Justice also leads the administration of the entire Judicial Branch. The Court interprets the Constitution and decides cases involving serious legal questions.

Court of Appeals

The Court of Appeals is the intermediate appellate court between the Courts-Martial and the Supreme Court. It reviews final judgments, orders, and resolutions of the Courts-Martial and administrative decisions within its jurisdiction.

Courts-Martial

The Courts-Martial are the trial courts of the Judiciary under the exclusive authority and supervision of the Judiciary. They exercise original jurisdiction over grave and capital felonies and other matters assigned by law.
